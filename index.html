How much should you keep?

A common planning range is 3–6 months of essential expenses. People with variable income, dependants, or limited insurance may prefer a larger buffer.

Keep it accessiblePrioritise liquidity and capital preservation over high returns. An emergency fund is not meant to maximise investment performance.
Separate it from daily spendingConsider a dedicated savings account or another suitable low-risk, accessible option. Understand withdrawal rules and risks.
Revisit your targetUpdate it when rent, family responsibilities, income, or essential expenses change.
